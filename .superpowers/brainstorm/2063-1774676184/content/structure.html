Landing page structure

Which rhythm feels right for a credibility surface? Think Anthropic, Stripe, or a serious research lab — not a blog or demo showcase.

A

Cinematic reveal

Full-viewport hero with the screenshot, name, one sentence, and proof numbers. Nothing else above the fold. Scroll down to find expandable sections: "The instrument", "Evidence", "Architecture", "About". Each is a single-line heading that opens on click.

Like: Anthropic's research pages, Linear's landing. Maximum restraint.

B

Layered descent

Clean hero (not full-viewport, maybe 60-70vh). Below it, 3-4 tight sections that are always visible but extremely concise — one heading, one image or embed, one sentence each. No cards, no grids, no multi-paragraph explanations. You see everything by scrolling, but there's very little to see per section.

Like: Vercel's product pages. Everything visible but nothing verbose.

C

Tabbed depth

Clean hero, then a single horizontal tab bar below it: Overview | Instrument | Evidence | Architecture | About. Each tab replaces the content below without scrolling. The visitor chooses their depth. Page never feels long.

Like: Apple product pages with their nav anchors. Controlled, navigable.
