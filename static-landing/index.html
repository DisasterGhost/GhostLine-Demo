Mechanism

The instrument reads what the model is doing while it's still doing it.

Each forward pass through a transformer leaves a trajectory through high-dimensional activation space. GhostLine projects that trajectory, classifies the geometric signature of every token, and surfaces 58 derived signals — entropy, velocity, effective dimensionality, attention entropy, hallucination risk — at the moment of generation.

01 · CAPTURE

Hooks at every layer.

Forward-pass hooks capture residual stream, attention, and MLP activations per token. No gradient tape, no extra forward — instrument runs alongside inference.

02 · CLASSIFY

Geometric state, not text.

A trained classifier reads the activation geometry and labels each token with one of five cognitive states. Detection is content-independent.

03 · DERIVE

58 signals, per token.

Entropy, velocity, effective dimensionality, logit-lens distance, attention concentration, and 53 more. Streamed as time-series and queryable post-hoc.

Why now

Text-based monitoring can't see this. The geometric layer can.

Output filtering reads what the model said. GhostLine reads what the model was doing while saying it. That distinction is now the difference between catching prompt injection, satisfying EU AI Act behavioral monitoring requirements, and gating expensive chain-of-thought before it runs.

Compliance & Audit

A-01

Per-token behavioral state classification with replayable trajectory logs. Continuous monitoring artifacts that regulators can inspect directly — not post-hoc reports synthesized from output text.

EU AI Act · Art 9 EU AI Act · Art 12 NIST AI RMF

Inference Optimization

A-02

Pre-generation prediction identifies prompts likely to fabricate before any tokens are produced. Route, re-prompt, or gate chain-of-thought in ~15 ms — before wasting compute on bad output.

Defense & Security

A-03

Geometric monitoring is independent of output content. An adversary can craft text that passes content filters but cannot easily control the geometry of the model's computation. Prompt injection screening at the activation level.
